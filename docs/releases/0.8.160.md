# OpenSCCT 0.8.160

Original PS2 maps retain their source world-geometry collision rule through explicit map metadata. The Hospital staircase beneath the Rooms 200 / Lift signs now uses its existing collision mesh. Content pack 1.0.2 supplies this marker for all fourteen PS2 maps.

PS2 objective consoles use the case as their facing target, keeping the normal Use prompt available when a spy approaches the cabinet. Interaction RPC phases retain their order behind earlier movement-state commands, so a valid remote switch hack reaches the authority.

An explicit SetCollision call made before physics binding now remains authoritative. This preserves the original sabotage targets' disabled collision instead of restoring their large imported cylinder blockers. On Deftech Belew, both lifts can reach the upper stop and open the upper doors; the permitted standing exit routes can open and be crossed.

Hospital content 1.0.2 restores the three beam meshes' cooked vertex colours and the proved normal-view fixed additive coefficient. Standard and Fast show the surrounding wall through the faded shaft instead of a broad white rectangle. A substantial saturated central patch remains unresolved; this update does not fully resolve the Hospital lighting report. This is a source-backed correction, not a claim of pixel parity with a captured PS2 frame.

The launcher remains version 0.8.63. PC map defaults and original PS2 team restrictions, hack requirements and encroachment retry rules are preserved.

## Gameplay checks and limits

Focused rendered checks use normal player input after fixture positioning, with a separate replicated authority and client world: Hospital stair ascent/descent, close console approach and hack, both Deftech lifts and their upper doors, standing exit routes, and Extraction, Neutralization and Sabotage objective regressions. These are local networked checks, not complete matches or a separate-machine internet session.

A crouched spy continuously pressed against one slanted Deftech exit face can make the door retry on a tiny actual overlap. Source investigation found the same encroachment policy and mesh pullback; no unsupported tolerance or pawn exemption was added. A normal backstep, wait and forward movement recovered all three crouched exit routes in the headless replicated check.

Cinema's eight authored glass-door panels were profiled with normal approach and hacking at 2560 by 1440 in Standard and Fast, with native audio mixing active. Occasional frame hitches occurred, but the reported sustained extreme slowdown was not reproduced on the test RTX 4070 SUPER. No speculative Cinema performance fix is included. Different hardware, cold shader compilation and an external online session remain untested.

The Cinema report concerns Fast. The exact triggering panel and reporting player's hardware remain unconfirmed.
