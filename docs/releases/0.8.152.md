New merc weapons, browser play, and gameplay fixes.

- Added the M4, AK-47, Galil and FAMAS, with aim-down sights, an M4 red-dot sight, individual grips and reloads, and quarter-speed movement while aiming.
- Added five sidearms with a dedicated weapon slot. Equipment screens show weapon renders, descriptions, fire rates and head/body damage. Gun damage falls with distance.
- Reworked the AK reload and revolver cylinder/case handling. Restored mine-placement gestures on custom weapons and fixed the stretched left hand after grenade launches.
- Restored stock gun sounds and fixed the M4's tactical reload sound after an empty reload. Improved positional audio, obstruction, room reverb and headphone rendering, and fixed loud effect-tail noise.
- Added spy-clone and mine-hacking gadgets, clone recharge with a filling HUD indicator, and expanded practice/loadout controls. Fixed regular gadget use, quick-launch bindings and unintended clone activation.
- Restored the backpack's own/team gadget availability display. Fixed the laser reticle flashing during reloads and rifle ADS movement/idle transitions.
- Fixed duplicate/scaled foreground weapons in EMF, camera-edge corona artifacts, and improved bloom, projector precision and moving character shadows.
- Fixed presence-controlled doors reopening and closing as players enter/leave, geometric events attached to movers, and movement/animation handling under high latency.
- Added browser play through WebGPU, shared account/settings and multiplayer support, downloadable maps, and in-game vision-cancelling and grabbing lessons. The wiki also retains its earlier guided vision exercise.
- Added a practice-only grab-area overlay and weapon animation authoring workspaces.
