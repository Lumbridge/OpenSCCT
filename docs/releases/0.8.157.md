Makes maps with their own game modes discoverable in the regular map picker.

- Installed maps stay visible when the currently selected mode is unavailable on them. Selecting a map chooses one of its supported modes and loads its rules.
- Restores the original PS2 maps to the normal Create Session search, including Station P (GAR09P).
- Lists the original Spy Training P and Mercenary Training P layouts in their side's training browser and launches them on the correct side. These single-team layouts stay out of versus choices.

The two recovered training layouts are playable through that browser; this does not establish full parity with the original PS2 tutorial scripting and progression.

Launcher version: 0.8.63.
