OpenSCCT realistic merc rifles
=============================

The four original rifle models and the shared arms use CC BY 4.0.
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

M4A1-S model and draw/fire/reload/holster animation reference:
This work is based on "M4 - FPS Weapon Animations Pack V.1"
(https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/m4-fps-weapon-animations-pack-v1-662fc74dda2646cfb48fc610705768ef)
by BarcodeGames (https://sketchfab.com/barcodegames), licensed under CC-BY-4.0.
Changes: hip-fire camera framing; clip trimming; optimized external textures;
an original OpenSCCT steel suppressor; adaptation of its handling to all four rifles.

Shared first-person cloth sleeves and gloves:
This work is based on "animated pistol"
(https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/animated-pistol-bd896167e7ca44f19597d3afe6a8d83f)
by DJMaesen (https://sketchfab.com/bumstrum), licensed under CC-BY-4.0.
Changes: the same mesh and atlas used by OpenSCCT sidearms; rifle contact fitting;
fixed-length arm solves and distributed forearm twist; authored contextual actions.

AK-47:
This work is based on "AK47"
(https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/ak47-831519a097d84e079fd8bc4b15e5b57d)
by wburton (https://sketchfab.com/wburton95), licensed under CC-BY-4.0.
Changes: normalized axes/scale; original animated magazine/bolt separated;
unused ammunition/spare parts removed; fitted to the shared cloth arms.

GALIL:
This work is based on "IMI Galil weapon"
(https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/imi-galil-weapon-c04464da23af47458533a8a8c74f4b4b)
by MilotoR1 (https://sketchfab.com/m1lotor), licensed under CC-BY-4.0.
Changes: normalized axes/scale; seven 4K material sets resized to 1K; magazine attached
to the credited animation rig; support-hand contact and material response adapted.

FAMAS:
This work is based on "FAMAS"
(https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/famas-7d35e14ad8874d148ce9cc46e5765a6b)
by Frostoise (https://sketchfab.com/Frostoise), licensed under CC-BY-4.0.
Changes: normalized axes/scale; magazine attached to the credited animation
rig with a bullpup well offset; support-hand contact and matte polymer response adapted.

Every rifle includes an original OpenSCCT weapon attachment rig, muzzle and
case-ejection joints, texture optimization and menu renders. Contextual SCCT
actions use the user's converted clip names and durations during installation;
no retail model or animation is included in these distributable exports.

Audio
-----
Free Firearm Sound Library by Ben Jaszczak, Brian Nelson, Kevin Heras and
Matthew Nanney. CC0-1.0, https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
Mirror: https://github.com/buddingmonkey/FreeFirearmsSFXLibrary
AK-47 and AR-15 field recordings, isolated to individual reports and adapted
with mono conversion, equalization, pitch/gain changes and faded tails.

Gun reload sounds (rifle handling) by SpringySpringo, CC0-1.0.
https://opengameart.org/content/gun-reload-sounds
PistolReloadSound.wav by MaximBomba, CC0-1.0.
https://freesound.org/people/MaximBomba/sounds/432139/
silenced pistol shot (.45 reference) by Clutvh, CC0-1.0.
https://freesound.org/people/Clutvh/sounds/627087/
Handling and suppressed report layers use the existing credited source
recordings in content/assets/weapons and content/assets/sidearms.
Reports are game sound designs; Galil/FAMAS/M4 suppressor layers are adapted
references rather than exact recordings of each displayed configuration.

No Valve/Counter-Strike assets, paid content or AI-generated gun assets used.

Original OpenSCCT additions (MIT): compact tubular red-dot optic, its riser and fasteners; FAMAS sight plates/blade fitted into the native carry-handle channel (visual reference: French service manual MAT 1057, figures 5/6); individual AK/Galil hand contacts and reload handling; sight calibration. Generated by author_sight.py, svmbuild/rifle_handling.py and svmbuild/rifle_contacts.py.
