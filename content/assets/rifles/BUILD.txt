Realistic merc rifle assets
==========================

All four downloads are identified, attributed and SHA-256 fingerprinted in
sources.json. Obtain the max-resolution GLB download from each model's
Sketchfab page, retaining its listed downloadFile name in one source folder.
The Galil download is large; it intentionally stays outside the repository.

Rebuild distributable art with Python/numpy/Pillow:
  python content/assets/rifles/author.py --sources <download-folder>

Rebuild the transparent equipment icons with Blender 5.x:
  blender --background --disable-autoexec --python content/assets/rifles/render.py

Rebuild the original OpenSCCT tube optic:
  blender --background --disable-autoexec --python content/assets/rifles/author_sight.py

The chars step retains the M4/FAMAS authored reloads exactly. AK and Galil each
use separate fore-end contacts and reload treatment in rifle_contacts.py.
The imported FAMAS omits its sight plates/blade: the chars step completes these
inside its native carry-handle channel, using MAT 1057 figures 5/6 as a visual reference.
https://www.kalasnyikov.hu/dokumentumok/famas.pdf
It also mounts sights on the first- and third-person rigs and generates matching
stock/M4/FAMAS side renders with Python/numpy/Pillow; Blender is not a content-build dependency.

Rebuild the eight audio files with Python/numpy/ffmpeg:
  python content/assets/rifles/author_audio.py

The checked-in exports contain only external licensed art and OpenSCCT's own
rig/geometry work. The normal chars content step copies these exports and
retargets contextual SCCT motions from the player's locally converted rifle.
The audio content step installs rifles.json and its credited recording bank.
Do not copy locally assembled *_first.glb files back into export/.

Balance lives in src/OpenScct.Game/Equipment/MercRifles.cs. All four use the
stock trace, hit-zone, movement-spread, recoil and distance-falloff systems.
Initial close damage / interval / magazine / reserve / reload:
  M4A1-S  22 / .100 / 20 / 80  / 2.40 s; .4 noise radius, red dot
  AK-47   29 / .100 / 30 / 90  / 2.80 s; strongest recoil, iron sights
  GALIL   21 / .090 / 35 / 105 / 3.00 s; wider spread, iron sights
  FAMAS   22 / .090 / 25 / 100 / 2.65 s; burst by default (.075 within burst), iron sights
Damage is before hit-zone scaling. These are initial sidegrade estimates;
manual spy-versus-merc and connected-client play are required for tuning.
Rates and exponential damage retention use CS2's weapons.vdata as a reference:
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/SteamTracking/GameTracking-CS2/master/game/csgo/pak01_dir/scripts/weapons.vdata
Damage retention per 12.7 metres: M4A1-S .94, AK/Galil .98, FAMAS .96.
Use the existing Scope binding for rifle ADS; hold and toggle scope bindings both work.
