Sidearm firing and reload audio
===============================
Recordings downloaded from the public high-quality previews / downloads linked below.
Changes by OpenSCCT: mono 44.1 kHz / 16-bit PCM; one shot isolated per report; linear gain; short faded tails. Reload handling transients cut and placed at authored animation cue times (export/audio/timing.json).
No dynamics compressor or limiter. The Glock firing recording is unchanged.
