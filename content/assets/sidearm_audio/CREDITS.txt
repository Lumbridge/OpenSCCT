OpenSCCT sidearm audio refresh
=============================

Free Firearm Sound Library by Ben Jaszczak, Brian Nelson, Kevin Heras and
Matthew Nanney, CC0-1.0.
https://github.com/buddingmonkey/FreeFirearmsSFXLibrary
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
Pinned revision: beb2f4041f3d6740fa0aeaf0e71159bd65a78c1b

Standard pistol: near 1911 .45 ACP reports (A_42P).
Automatic pistol: near Walther PPQ 9 mm reports (X_39P).
Suppressed pistol: Marvel suppressed .22 reports from behind the shooter (J_1),
with a restrained low-frequency .45 reference layer. This is an adapted USP
sound design, not a recording of a suppressed USP.
Magazine/slide handling: 1911 (A_4, A_5, A_9) and Walther PPQ (X_3, X_4, X_9).
Revolver handling: Smith & Wesson 642 (V_0, V_1, V_2, V_3), adapted to the
existing six-round loader animation. These mechanisms are reference layers.
Original file URLs, byte counts and SHA-256 hashes: sources/sources.json.

.44 Magnum report: 44 Magnum.wav by dcsimon, with the existing
IDontLikeFruit / LUDATOR Interactive field-recording edit.
https://freesound.org/people/dcsimon/sounds/139979/
https://idontlikefruit.com/audio/v1/shot-pome-53bd87e23d0c.wav
https://idontlikefruit.com/audio/CREDITS.html
CC-BY-3.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/
Source retained in content/assets/sidearms/sources/audio/magnum44_field.wav.

Desert Eagle report: Desert Eagle .50 by AugustSandberg.
https://freesound.org/people/AugustSandberg/sounds/231243/
CC0-1.0, https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
Source retained in content/assets/sidearms/sources/audio/desert_eagle.mp3.

OpenSCCT edits: choose a clean microphone channel, isolate direct attacks,
remove DC/subsonic rumble, adjust spectral balance, shorten environmental tails,
add quiet recorded action layers, normalize linear peak gain, and author three
variations per sidearm. Variations use independent recorded shots where present;
single-take heavy reports also use pitch changes below one percent.
No rifle-shot layer, synthetic explosion, compressor or limiter is used.
Reload cues retain the existing animation and gameplay durations.

Rebuild: python content/assets/sidearm_audio/author.py (numpy, ffmpeg/ffprobe).
The audio content step installs this bank after the older weapon overlay,
so OpenScctSidearms.* resolves to the refreshed reports and reloads.
