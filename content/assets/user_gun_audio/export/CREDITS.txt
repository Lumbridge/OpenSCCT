OpenSCCT user-supplied Gun SFX bank
=================================

Source: Gun SFX folder supplied by the user for this project.
Original creator and licence: not stated in the supplied folder.
No Creative Commons licence is attributed to these files.
Original relative filenames and SHA-256 hashes: sources/manifest.json.

USP40 -> suppressed and standard sidearms.
Glock18 -> automatic sidearm and stock SMG reference.
Revolver -> .44 Magnum sidearm reference.
Desert Eagle -> Desert Eagle sidearm.
552 Commando suppressed -> M4A1-S reference.
552 Commando unsuppressed -> Galil reference.
AK47 and FAMAS -> their matching rifle profiles.
AUG A3 -> stock assault rifle reference.
Benelli M3 -> stock shotgun, shell and pump references.

Changes: mono 44.1 kHz PCM conversion, direct attack isolation, linear peak gain,
short tail fades, and reload contacts cut from the supplied composites and
placed at the existing authored cue times. Individual shots are played by the
game's firing cadence; no recorded bursts or automatic loops are layered over it.

Rebuild from the retained sources: python content/assets/user_gun_audio/author.py
Initial import: append --source followed by the Gun SFX folder path.
